How ChatRecall AI Works

1. How Conversations Are Recalled

When you enter a subject (e.g. Computer Communication Networks), ChatRecall AI generates related technical keywords (TCP/IP, OSI model, routing, congestion control). It then scans your active conversation history across ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity tabs in Chrome.

2. Honest Access & Platform Status

Verified Match Found in your active signed-in session history.
Tab Not Open Platform tab is not open in Chrome. Click "Open Platform" to launch it.
Manual Fallback Direct link to platform's native search when deep scraping is restricted.

3. Privacy Guarantee

  • Zero Credentials Stored: ChatRecall AI never accesses your password, API keys, or session tokens.
  • 100% Local Execution: All keyword expansion and filtering runs directly inside your Chrome browser. No external telemetry or backend servers are used.
  • No Fabricated Links: Only genuine, verified conversation links are presented.
